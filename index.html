OH&S Policies CRAFT-OHS-POL-001

1. Purpose & Scope

CraftOS is committed to providing a safe and healthy working environment for all employees, contractors, and visitors across all remote mining and industrial client sites.

2. Field Operator Responsibilities

All trades personnel must adhere strictly to Take 5 hazard assessments, cardinal safety rules, and correct PPE standards at all times during operational swings.

3. Incident Reporting

All near-misses and hazardous conditions must be reported to the on-site HSE supervisor immediately and logged through this portal.
